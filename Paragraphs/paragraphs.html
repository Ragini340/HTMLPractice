Bajaj replaced the Dominar 400's 373 cc engine with a 349.13 cc liquid-cooled single-cylinder unit, also used in the Pulsar NS400Z. It retains a high compression ratio and gains ride-by-wire with four riding modes Road, Rain, Sport and Off-Road. Power and torque figures remain close to before, but the delivery is more engaging at high revs and less urgent at low speeds.

The same 349 cc engine is reported to feel smoother and more tractable in the lighter Pulsar NS400Z, which benefits from sharper handling and better vibration control. The NS400Z's lighter weight and sportier chassis make it more engaging in varied riding conditions. This contrast suggests the Dominar's heavier build may limit the potential of the updated engine.
