Maruti Suzuki XL7

Suzuki has released the first official image of the facelifted XL7 ahead of its 29 July debut at GIIAS 2026. The model, already sold in ASEAN markets, features a redesigned chrome grille with black inserts, sharper headlights, new fog lamp housings, updated alloy wheels, and smoked LED tail lamps. These updates are expected to carry over to the Indian Maruti XL6 facelift arriving later this year.
